About Me

I am a Computer Science student with a deep passion for data visualization and brand identity. I believe that data shouldn't just be accurate—it should be beautiful and easy to understand.

My work bridges the gap between technical analysis and creative design. Whether I'm building interactive dashboards, designing print media, or organizing data models, I focus on delivering clarity and impact.
